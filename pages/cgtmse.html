Conference Theme: Shaping the Future Through Global Collaboration

The 38th ACSIC Conference is organized around a unifying theme that captures the essence of our times: "Shaping the future through global collaboration, risk sustainability, and technological innovation."

Why This Theme?

As we navigate an increasingly complex and interconnected world, we face unprecedented challenges—from climate change and economic inequality to pandemic preparedness and geopolitical tensions. These challenges demand a reimagining of how we work together across borders, sectors, and disciplines.

Core Pillars

Our theme rests on three foundational pillars:

  • Global Collaboration: Breaking silos and fostering partnerships that span nations and sectors
  • Risk Sustainability: Building resilient systems that balance economic, social, and environmental needs
  • Technological Innovation: Leveraging digital transformation and emerging technologies for good

Conference Objectives

Through this conference, we aim to:

  • Foster meaningful dialogue among global leaders and innovators
  • Share evidence-based insights on emerging trends and best practices
  • Identify collaborative solutions to shared challenges
  • Build lasting networks and partnerships
  • Generate actionable recommendations for policymakers and practitioners
