About the Venue

The Taj Mahal Palace, Mumbai

The Taj Mahal Palace, Mumbai

Situated in the heart of Mumbai at Apollo Bunder, The Taj Mahal Palace stands as an iconic symbol of luxury and elegance. Built in 1903, this grand hotel has been host to world leaders, diplomats, and distinguished guests for over a century. With its distinctive dome architecture and prime waterfront location, it represents the perfect setting for the Global Symposium & 38th ACSIC Conference 2026.

World-Class Facilities

The Taj Mahal Palace offers state-of-the-art conference facilities designed to meet the highest international standards. The venue features multiple auditoriums and meeting spaces equipped with modern audiovisual technology, high-speed internet, and advanced security systems. Our conference will benefit from:

  • Large multi-purpose auditoriums capable of hosting 2000+ participants
  • Breakout rooms for parallel sessions and workshops
  • Professional registration and information areas
  • Networking lounges and dining facilities
  • Complimentary WiFi throughout the venue
  • Dedicated support staff and on-site services

Location & Accessibility

Located in the vibrant business and cultural district of South Mumbai, the venue is easily accessible from major airports and transportation hubs. The Apollo Bunder area is home to numerous hotels, restaurants, and cultural attractions, making it ideal for conference delegates who wish to explore the city.

Accommodation & Amenities

The hotel features over 500 luxurious rooms and suites offering stunning views of the Arabian Sea. Guests can enjoy world-class dining options, spa facilities, fitness center, and recreational amenities. Special conference rates are available for all registered participants.
