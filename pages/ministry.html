Government Partnership & Support

Ministry Support for the Conference

The Global Symposium & 38th ACSIC Conference 2026 is supported by various government ministries and departments committed to sustainable development and international cooperation. This government endorsement reflects the strategic importance of the topics being discussed at the conference.

Key Government Partners

The conference has received support from ministries responsible for:

  • Finance and Economic Development
  • Foreign Affairs and International Relations
  • Commerce and Industry
  • Environment and Sustainability
  • Technology and Innovation

Policy Framework

The conference aligns with national and international development agendas, including the Sustainable Development Goals and regional development frameworks. Government participation ensures that discussions translate into actionable policies and practical implementation strategies.

Facilitating Participation

Government support includes facilitating visa arrangements for international participants, coordinating logistics, and ensuring the conference addresses policy priorities at the highest levels.
