Accompanying Persons

Programs and Activities for Guests

Special Programs for Accompanying Persons

We understand that many conference participants will be accompanied by family members. To make the conference experience more enjoyable for all, we have arranged special programs and activities.

Daily Activities

Accompanying persons can enjoy curated activities including shopping tours in Mumbai, wellness sessions at the hotel spa, recreational activities, and cultural experiences. Special rates are available for hotel services.

Evening Events

All accompanying persons are welcome to join evening receptions and social events, providing opportunities to meet other guests and explore the vibrant culture of Mumbai.

Registration & Support

Special accompanying person passes can be arranged during conference registration. Hotel concierge staff will be available to assist with recommendations for tours, dining, and entertainment throughout your stay.
