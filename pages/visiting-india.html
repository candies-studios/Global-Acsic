Visiting India

Essential Information for International Visitors

Welcome to India

India is a diverse and vibrant nation with a rich history, diverse cultures, and warm hospitality. As an international conference participant, we want to ensure you have a smooth and enjoyable visit.

Visa Requirements

Most international visitors require a visa to enter India. We recommend applying for your visa at least 2-3 months before the conference. Our conference organizers can provide invitation letters to support your visa application. E-visas are available for many nationalities and can be obtained online.

Health & Safety

India is generally safe for international visitors. However, we recommend consulting your doctor about any vaccinations or health precautions before traveling. Comprehensive travel insurance is advised. Medical facilities are available throughout Mumbai with several world-class hospitals.

Currency & Banking

The currency in India is the Indian Rupee (INR). ATMs are widely available in Mumbai for withdrawing cash. Most hotels, restaurants, and shops accept credit cards. International wire transfers can be arranged through major banks.

Weather & Packing

April in Mumbai is warm with temperatures around 28-32°C (82-90°F). Light, breathable clothing is recommended. Don't forget sunscreen, sunglasses, and comfortable walking shoes. Light rain is possible, so a light jacket or umbrella is advisable.

Communication

India uses 230V AC electricity with Type D plugs. International roaming is available from most mobile networks. WiFi is widely available in hotels and public areas. India's country code is +91.
