About Mumbai

The City of Dreams & Gateway to India

Mumbai: A Vibrant Global Metropolis

Mumbai, the capital of Maharashtra, is India's premier financial, commercial, and entertainment hub. With a population of over 20 million people, it is one of the world's most densely populated cities and a vital center for international business, culture, and trade. As the birthplace of Bollywood and home to some of Asia's most iconic landmarks, Mumbai seamlessly blends tradition with modernity.

Historical Significance

Mumbai's history spans over 4,000 years. Originally a collection of fishing villages, the city was developed by Portuguese traders in the 16th century and later became a major British colonial settlement. The British transformed Mumbai into a world-class port city, leaving behind architectural marvels like the Gateway of India, Victoria Terminus, and the Taj Mahal Palace. Today, these landmarks stand as testaments to the city's rich colonial heritage and its evolution into a modern metropolis.

Economy & Business

Mumbai is the economic powerhouse of India, generating nearly 6% of the country's GDP. The city hosts the headquarters of major corporations, financial institutions, and banking centers. The Mumbai Stock Exchange (BSE), one of the world's oldest and largest stock exchanges, is located here. The city's dynamic business environment attracts entrepreneurs and investors from across the globe, making it a crucial player in global commerce.

Culture & Arts

Mumbai is synonymous with Indian cinema and entertainment. Bollywood, based in Mumbai, produces thousands of films annually and is watched by audiences worldwide. Beyond cinema, the city hosts numerous art galleries, museums, theaters, and cultural events. The city's cosmopolitan character is reflected in its diverse cuisine, festivals, and artistic expressions that blend Indian, Islamic, Hindu, and Western influences.

Key Attractions & Landmarks

  • Gateway of India: A 20-meter stone arch built in 1924, it's the city's most iconic landmark and a popular gathering spot
  • Victoria Terminus (VT): A UNESCO World Heritage Site, this Gothic-Victorian architecture masterpiece now serves as the central railway station
  • The Taj Mahal Palace: Built in 1903, this luxurious hotel is an architectural icon overlooking the Arabian Sea
  • Elephanta Caves: Ancient rock-cut Hindu and Buddhist caves on an island, containing intricate sculptures dating back 5-8 centuries
  • Marine Drive: A scenic 3.6 km long waterfront boulevard offering stunning sea views and a vibrant nightlife scene
  • Hanging Gardens: Terraced gardens offering panoramic views of the city and Arabian Sea
  • Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Vastu Sangrahalaya: India's premier museum housing extensive collections of art, textiles, and antiquities
  • Bandra-Worli Sea Link: An architectural marvel connecting Bandra and Worli, offering breathtaking views

Food & Cuisine

Mumbai is a food lover's paradise. From street food like vada pav, pani puri, and bhel puri to fine dining restaurants offering international cuisine, the city caters to all culinary preferences. The famous Mumbai street food scene is an integral part of the city's identity. Fresh seafood, South Indian delicacies, and regional specialties are readily available throughout the city.

Climate & Best Time to Visit

Mumbai experiences a tropical climate with three main seasons: Summer (March-May) is hot and humid with temperatures reaching 32-35°C. The monsoon season (June-September) brings heavy rainfall and cooler temperatures. Winter (October-February) is pleasant with temperatures ranging from 15-25°C. April, when our conference is held, is perfect for visiting as the weather is warm but manageable, and the monsoon season is just beginning.

Accommodation & Dining

Mumbai offers accommodation options ranging from luxury five-star hotels to budget-friendly guesthouses. Major hotel chains like Taj Hotels, ITC, Oberoi, and Hilton have properties in the city. The restaurant scene is equally diverse, with everything from traditional Indian cuisine to modern fusion restaurants. South Mumbai, where the conference venue is located, has particularly high concentrations of excellent dining and accommodation options.

Shopping & Entertainment

Shopping in Mumbai ranges from traditional markets like Crawford Market and Chor Bazaar to modern shopping malls like Bandra Kurla Complex. High Street Phoenix, Westside, and other malls offer international brands. For entertainment, the city has numerous cinema halls, theaters, bars, and nightclubs. The vibrant nightlife scene and abundant entertainment options make Mumbai an exciting destination for visitors.

Practical Information

  • Time Zone: IST (Indian Standard Time), UTC+5:30
  • Language: Marathi (local language), Hindi, English widely spoken
  • Currency: Indian Rupee (₹); 1 USD ≈ 83-85 INR (approximate)
  • Emergency Services: Police: 100, Ambulance: 102, Fire: 101
  • International Airport: Bombay High (Indira Gandhi International style facility)
  • Public Transport: Metro, buses, taxis, and auto-rickshaws readily available

Upcoming Events in April 2026

April is a vibrant month in Mumbai with various cultural events and celebrations. Visitors to the conference will have opportunities to experience local festivals, cultural performances, and special events happening throughout the city. Check with the hotel concierge or conference staff for recommendations on events and activities during your stay.

Final Thoughts

Mumbai is a city of contradictions—ancient and modern, chaotic and organized, local and global. It's a place where tradition thrives alongside innovation, where dreams are pursued, and where people from all backgrounds come together. We are delighted to host the Global Symposium & 38th ACSIC Conference in this dynamic and inspiring city. We hope you'll take time during your visit to explore Mumbai's rich culture, history, and vibrant energy.
