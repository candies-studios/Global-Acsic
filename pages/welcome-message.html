Welcome Message

Message from the Conference Organizers

Welcome to Global Symposium & 38th ACSIC Conference 2026

We are delighted to welcome you to the Global Symposium & 38th ACSIC Conference 2026, a premier international platform bringing together distinguished leaders, policymakers, academics, and development professionals from across the globe.

A Unique Platform for Global Dialogue

This conference serves as a unique gathering where diverse perspectives converge to address pressing challenges and explore unprecedented opportunities in global development. Over the course of two days, we will engage in thought-provoking discussions, share cutting-edge research, and foster meaningful collaborations that transcend borders and sectors.

Key Themes and Focus Areas

Our conference is structured around three pivotal themes: Global Collaboration & Partnership, Risk Management & Sustainability, and Innovation & Technology. These themes reflect the contemporary imperatives facing our world and the need for integrated, multidisciplinary approaches to sustainable development.

A Commitment to Excellence

We have meticulously curated a program featuring renowned international experts, distinguished panel discussions, and networking sessions designed to foster lasting professional relationships. The venue, the iconic Taj Mahal Palace in Mumbai, provides an inspiring setting for these critical conversations.

Your Participation Matters

Whether you are a government official shaping policy, a financial sector leader driving investment, an industry professional fostering innovation, an academic researcher advancing knowledge, or a development worker implementing change on the ground—your presence and insights are invaluable to our collective success.

Looking Forward

We look forward to welcoming you to Mumbai in April 2026. Together, we will explore the pathways toward a more sustainable, prosperous, and interconnected world. We are confident that this conference will leave a lasting impact on your professional journey and contribute meaningfully to global progress.

Warm regards,
Conference Organizing Committee
